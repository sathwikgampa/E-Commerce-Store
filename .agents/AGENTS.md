# Sri Thirumala E-Commerce Redesign Rules

## Color Palette & Typography Guidelines
- **Primary Brand Color:** Warm Orange (`#FF7A50`) is used for primary actions, titles, highlights, badges, buttons, and links across all consumer-facing pages.
- **Secondary Accent:** Soft Peach (`#FFF7F5`, `#FFEFEA`) is used for card backgrounds, highlights, and secondary states.
- **Typography:** The *Lora* font is loaded and should be applied to major page headings to maintain the premium, editorial bookstore aesthetic.
- **Footer & Utility Areas:** Dark charcoal `#2D3748` / `#1A1A2E` is preferred over blue for structured header and footer sections.

## Navigation Redirects
- Clicking the brand logo in the header must navigate to `/home` (the bookstore storefront homepage) instead of `/` (the animated landing page).

## Admin Portal Exception
- **Exception:** The admin portal dashboard and backend inventory pages (`/admin/...`) should retain their dark navy blue (`#0A3D91`) color styling to separate the storefront presentation from back-office management.
